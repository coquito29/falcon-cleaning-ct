### 6. Verification
Google will ask to verify. The most common option for service-area businesses:
- **Postcard by mail** (5–7 days) — comes to her physical address with a code, type it in
- **Video verification** (sometimes offered) — record a short video showing the business is real
- **Phone call** (sometimes offered) — automated call with code

Pick whichever Google offers. **Postcard is the most reliable.**

## Step 8 — Get her first 5 reviews (this week)

Reviews are the **#1 ranking factor**. After verification, share this link with past customers:

`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=PLACEID`

(Replace `PLACEID` with the one Google gives her after the listing is published — it's in the share menu of her dashboard.)

**Text to send to past customers:**

## Step 9 — Post weekly on the GBP

Just like Facebook, Google Business Profile has "Posts." Post once a week:
- Special offers ("$25 off first cleaning this month!")
- Photos of recent jobs
- Tips ("How to keep your Airbnb 5-star ready")
- Service announcements

This signals to Google she's active, which boosts ranking.
