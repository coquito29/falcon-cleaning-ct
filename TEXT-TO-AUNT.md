Send the SHORT first text, wait for her to reply, then send the FOLLOW-UP.

## 📋 What to text her LATER (the GBP guide)

When she's ready to do step 3 (Google Business Profile claim), send her the contents of `GBP-CLAIM-GUIDE.md` — that's the step-by-step she follows on her own time.

You can copy-paste the whole guide as a long text, or send it as a series of shorter texts (each step on its own message).

---

## 💡 Best practice

1. **Send Step 1 text NOW** — gets her excited, hooks her with the URL
2. **Wait for her reply** — could be minutes or hours
3. **Send Step 2 follow-up** — the 3 asks, framed as small/easy
4. **When she's ready for GBP** — text her the guide
5. **Stay available via text** for any questions

This drip approach (vs. one giant email) feels less overwhelming and gets faster responses.
