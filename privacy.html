Privacy & Terms

Last updated: May 2026

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you fill out our contact form or call us, we collect only what you give us — your name, email address, phone number, and details about the cleaning you'd like done. We don't track you across the web, sell your data, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your request, prepare an estimate, schedule service, and keep in touch about your cleanings. We may keep records of past services so we know what you like and how you prefer your home cleaned.

How we store it

Form submissions arrive at our business email (falconcleaningservicellc@gmail.com) and are kept only as long as needed to serve you. You can ask us to delete your records at any time — just call or email.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The site is hosted on Render and the contact map is embedded from Google Maps; both may set their own technical cookies.

Your rights

You can request a copy of any information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at the email or phone number on the home page.

Terms of Service

Estimates

All estimates are free and non-binding. Final pricing depends on the size of the space, condition, and any extras requested. We'll always confirm pricing before the first cleaning.

Scheduling & cancellations

We do our best to honor scheduled appointments. If you need to reschedule or cancel, please give us at least 24 hours' notice when possible. Same-day cancellations may be subject to a fee depending on circumstances.

Insurance & liability

Falcon Cleaning Service LLC is fully insured. In the unlikely event of damage caused by our team during a cleaning, please notify us within 48 hours so we can address it promptly.

Payment

Payment is due upon completion of each cleaning unless other arrangements have been made. We accept common payment methods — ask when scheduling.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Continued use of our services after a change means you accept the updated terms.
