Common questions

Got questions?

Quick answers to the things people ask most. Don't see yours? Just call or send a message.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes — we arrive fully equipped with professional-grade products and tools. If you have specific products you'd like us to use (eco-friendly, fragrance-free, etc.), just let us know.

How much does a clean cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the space, the type of clean (turnover, deep clean, recurring), and any extras. Every estimate is free and there's no obligation — call 860-705-4916 or fill out the form below.

Can you do same-day Airbnb turnovers?

Often, yes. We specialize in vacation rental turnovers and work with Airbnb, Guesty, and Zillow hosts. Same-day availability depends on our schedule — booking a recurring slot guarantees coverage.

Are you insured?

Yes, Falcon Cleaning Service is fully insured. You're protected on every visit.

What's included in a move-in / move-out clean?

A full top-to-bottom: kitchen (inside appliances, cabinets, counters, sinks), bathrooms (scrubbed and sanitized), all floors mopped or vacuumed, baseboards, walls spot-cleaned, dusting throughout, and trash removed. We leave the space ready for the next occupant.

Do you offer recurring service discounts?

Yes — qualifying repeat customers get a discount on every visit. Weekly, biweekly, and monthly schedules all qualify.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Most of our clients leave us a key, lockbox code, or smart-lock access. We'll lock up when we leave.

What towns do you serve?

We cover most of Southeastern Connecticut and parts of Rhode Island — see the full list above. If your town isn't listed, give us a call; we likely cover it.
