# Falcon Cleaning — TikTok Playbook

5 ready-to-record video concepts + hashtag strategy + caption templates.
Designed for your existing "ad TikTok" account, driving traffic to her business.

---

## Before you record: 4 ground rules

1. **First 3 seconds is everything.** Hook them with motion, a question, or a "wait what" moment. If they swipe in 2 sec, the algorithm buries the video.
2. **Vertical only.** 9:16 aspect ratio. Full screen.
3. **Always include text-on-screen.** 80% of TikTok is watched without sound.
4. **Caption MUST mention:** her name + phone + at least one CT town. That's how local viewers convert.

## 📊 Hashtag strategy (every post)

**Always include 3 of these "core" tags:**
- `#cleantok` (primary niche tag)
- `#cleaning`
- `#satisfying`

**Always include 2-3 LOCAL tags:**
- `#connecticut` or `#ct`
- `#mysticct` or `#newlondonct` or `#niantic` (whichever town the video is in)
- `#shoreline` or `#newengland`

**Add 2-3 specific tags based on video type:**
- Airbnb video → `#airbnb` `#airbnbhost` `#vacationrental`
- Move-out → `#moveoutcleaning` `#moving` `#securitydeposit`
- Hack → `#cleaninghacks` `#cleaningtips`
- Day in life → `#dayinthelife` `#smallbusiness` `#womenowned`

**Don't go above 8 tags** — TikTok says it doesn't matter, but real-world testing shows 5-8 hits the sweet spot.

## ⚠️ Things to avoid

- ❌ Long intros ("Hey guys, today I'm gonna...")
- ❌ Static talking-head (TikTok hates these)
- ❌ Watermarks from other apps (CapCut, Snapchat) — TikTok suppresses these
- ❌ More than 10 hashtags
- ❌ Generic captions ("Cleaning today!")
- ❌ Posting when you're not ready to engage with comments in the first hour (TikTok rewards early engagement)
